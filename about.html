About Anuj Singh (Code Hacker)

Python, AI and practical cybersecurity educator. Creator of the Code Hacker YouTube channel and the Code Hacker Academy platform.

Who I am

I'm Anuj Singh, known online as Code Hacker. I teach Python programming, AI projects and practical cybersecurity in Hindi/Hinglish, with a focus on learners who start from an Android phone and Termux — no laptop required to begin.

My goal is to make technical topics approachable: clear diagrams, exact commands, and step-by-step walkthroughs so you can learn, build, and verify things yourself.

Learning philosophy

Security demonstrations are for authorized learning and defense only. Practice on systems you own or have permission to test. Every tutorial emphasizes understanding how things work so you can protect your own accounts and systems.

I publish regularly on YouTube and keep the Academy updated with new courses and guides.
